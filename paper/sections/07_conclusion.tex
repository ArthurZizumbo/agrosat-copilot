\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

AgroSatCopilot shows that crop-area quantification from Sentinel-2 time
series is well served by a perceiver/reasoner design. Specialised
perception models --- temporal transformers, an XGBoost classifier over
AlphaEarth annual embeddings~\cite{brown2025alphaearth}, and a
contrastive vision-language branch~\cite{li2025farslip} --- are combined
through heterogeneous stacking, which improves the out-of-fold macro F1
over any individual model. The best individual perceiver, TSViT-pheno,
reaches mIoU 0.6253 and macro F1 0.7500 on the held-out fold, and the
five-member stacking ensemble attains an out-of-fold macro F1 of 0.6477.

The \emph{Be My Eyes} pattern~\cite{huang2025bemyeyes} establishes a
clean separation between the models that \emph{see} and a frozen
reasoner that \emph{communicates}, guaranteeing auditability and
resistance to hallucination, while a dual cloud/on-premises backend
preserves data sovereignty; the three interchangeable serving backends
answer an identical grounded query identically, differing only in
latency. The deployed production perceiver is the simpler Voting-3,
whose parcel-by-parcel inference reaches 97--99\% of in-scope parcels
correct across real French scenes while honestly abstaining on
out-of-scope crops.

Our multi-region transfer study is deliberately honest and now spans
five regions. Zero-shot generalisation fails (France to Catalonia mIoU
0.0000; even within-country France to Brittany drops to F1 0.21), and a
few-shot budget is what closes the gap; the foundation model lowers that
budget by roughly an order of magnitude in local labels. Transfer
\emph{does} work where phenology is separable (Lower Saxony DE4, macro
F1 0.266), and pooling regions widens the taxonomy from 18 to 30 leaves
without rescuing any fine class above 0.85 --- because the bottleneck is
phenological separability, not the number of regions. To build these
studies we released two self-built PASTIS-style datasets (Tuscany 2018
and Lower Saxony 2023).

The main outstanding challenge is the minority classes with low F1,
which require intra-annual temporal features or targeted augmentation
rather than more regions. The one genuinely pending item is the
quantitative conversational-layer quality benchmark on the H100
(US-069); the FarSLIP study (US-072) and the multi-region and DE4
transfer studies (US-073) are delivered in this paper.
